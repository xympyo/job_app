# CV strategy

The three CV records are first-class workspace entities. The stored `recommended_cv_id`
on a job is advice that Moshe can override; it is not a locked assignment. The import
format uses the names `Master`, `Analyst`, `Management/Product` or `Custom`, and resolves
the selected named CV to the owner's local record.

## The three narratives

### Master

Broad baseline and source of truth: technical problem-solving, manufacturing
digitalization, systems, automation, optimization and software delivery. Use when the
job spans several tracks, when evidence is incomplete, or when neither targeted story
clearly improves screening.

The repository seeds this record with the `master` slug. Its current code seed targets
Software Engineering, Automation and Solutions / Technical Consulting, while the
profile-level positioning remains broader.

### Analyst

Requirements → process analysis → systems/workflows → integration → measurable
improvement.

Default route for Business Analyst, IT Business Analyst, Business Systems Analyst,
System Analyst, Digital Transformation, Technology Consulting, ERP/implementation,
PMO/project analysis, Process Improvement, Operational Excellence, Corporate Planning,
and data-to-business or data-to-operations roles.

Use Analyst for work whose day-to-day substance is analysis, requirements, process,
systems, operations or transformation—even when the title is a graduate or officer
development program. An `ODP Data Analytics`-type role is not automatically Management.

### Management/Product

Leadership → business/product thinking → stakeholders → ownership → outcomes.

Default route for Management Trainee, Graduate Development, Future Leaders and similar
rotational programs, Associate Product Manager, Product Analyst and product-oriented
operations/project roles. It foregrounds leadership, coordination, stakeholder
communication, product judgment and trade-offs.

## Deterministic routing test

Read responsibilities and required work before reading the title. Ask:

1. Is the role mainly requirements/process/systems/data-to-operations work? Choose
   Analyst.
2. Is it mainly ownership, cross-functional leadership, product decisions or a genuine
   rotational management path? Choose Management/Product.
3. Is it primarily implementation/development/automation and neither targeted narrative
   is clearly better? Choose Master, or a truthful custom tailoring flag when warranted.
4. If the role spans tracks, choose the narrative that best matches the largest share of
   day-to-day work and explain the trade-off.

Do not route from title prestige, company name or program label alone. Record a short
reason tied to responsibilities. `Custom` means a small truthful change is useful; it
does not mean a new CV must be created before every application.

## Tailoring rule

Use an existing CV by default. Tailor only when the opportunity is high value and one
small, truthful change materially improves screening. Do not invent experience, rename
Homize as employment, or present the Mattel internship as full-time professional work.
LinkedIn remains a stable umbrella profile; the targeted CV carries role-specific emphasis.
