# Fit scoring

Fit is externally entered advice, never scientific probability or an automated gate.
Optional integer 0–100 must have a human explanation. Labels: Excellent Fit, Strong Fit,
Possible Fit, Stretch, Weak Fit, or unknown. No fabricated default scores.
Dimensions: role alignment, experience, qualification, career interest and practical fit.
Remote work does not automatically improve fit. See canonical profile for priorities.

Record why it fits, candidate-specific strengths, missing/uncertain requirements, red flags,
and recommendation: Apply ASAP, Apply, Apply if interested, Research first, Low priority,
Skip. Recommend Master, Analyst, Management/Product, or custom tailoring. All editable.
Show label prominently with score and explanation together. Do not auto-score from keywords.
# Bulk triage decisions

Triage results use four user-facing decisions: Apply ASAP, Apply, Research First and
Skip. They map to Ready to Apply, Ready to Apply, Reviewing and Skipped respectively;
the distinction between Apply ASAP and Apply remains in the existing recommendation
field. Fit and research fields are updated only when supplied by the triage result.
