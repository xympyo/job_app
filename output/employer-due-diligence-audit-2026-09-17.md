# Employer due diligence audit — 2026-09-17

## Scope and live snapshot

This was a read-only audit of the current owner workspace. No vacancy-search batch was
run and no records were written. The snapshot contained **45 companies, 47 jobs, 58 job
sources, 3 research runs, 14 applications (13 non-terminal and 1 Closed), 0 questions,
0 events and 3 CV variants**. The audit prioritised Preparing, Ready to Apply, Applied,
and realistic Found/Reviewing records. Closed/Skipped-only employers were retained as
historical context rather than re-researched in depth.

The source date for the public research below is 17 September 2026. Crowdsourced
reviews are directional signals, not verified facts; sample size and office/function
scope are included where available.

## Method

For each in-scope employer I checked available official/company identity evidence and
public employee-review or news sources in English and Indonesian where indexed. I looked
for repeated patterns in management, workload, development, compensation, contract
status, stability and role/team fit. I did not treat any star rating as a conclusion.
Where the public record was too thin or the company/role could not be matched reliably,
the result is HOLD / RESEARCH.

## Salary and benefits findings

The owner snapshot has no salary band for the audited roles. Public sources mostly provide
qualitative signals: BCA/BNI, Deliveree, Garena, Kapal Api and Traveloka reviews describe
competitive or above-average pay/benefits; Coway, Kearney, Summarecon, Friesland and
Traveloka include complaints about pay, raises, overtime or insurance. These are not
reliable offer ranges. Every interview should confirm base salary, THR/bonus, BPJS/private
insurance, allowances, overtime policy, probation, contract conversion and salary-review
timing in writing.

## Interview verification questions

Use the questions that match the concern; they are neutral and practical:

1. Who would I report to, and how is the team structured today?
2. What are the normal working hours, and how often do evenings or weekends occur?
3. How is overtime handled and compensated, if it occurs?
4. What would success look like in the first 90 days, and how are KPIs set and reviewed?
5. What is the employment status, probation length, and contract-to-permanent process?
6. How many people have held this role in the last two years, and why is it open now?
7. What onboarding, mentoring and training are guaranteed for a junior hire?
8. How are promotions and salary reviews decided, and when is the first review?
9. What percentage of the work is onsite, hybrid or travel, and where is the actual office?
10. For graduate programs, what team/role follows the rotation and what conversion is typical?
11. For consulting/logistics roles, what travel, client-site and peak-period expectations apply?
12. Which benefits are written into the offer: THR, BPJS, insurance, meal/transport allowance and bonus?

## Limitations

Review platforms are incomplete, anonymous and sometimes AI-summarised. Access was
not available to every login-gated page, and several small Indonesian employers have no
meaningful public employee sample. No conclusion should be generalized from a different
office, department, country or employment type. The next evidence step for HOLD records
is direct interview verification and checking the written offer, not another blind rating
search.
