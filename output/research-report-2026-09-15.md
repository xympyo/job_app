## Phase 1 — research and screening summary

I ran 18 query batches covering title and work variations such as business analyst, IT/business systems analyst, ERP/application consultant, digital transformation, process excellence, PMO/project analyst, product operations, corporate planning, PDCA, business process improvement and Indonesian equivalents including *analis bisnis*, *lulusan baru* and *fresh graduate*.

Sources searched: LinkedIn Jobs and recruiter/company posts; JobStreet Indonesia; Indeed Indonesia; Glints; Dealls; Kalibrr; Prosple and graduate portals; university/career pages; official company career pages; Google-indexed listings; and secondary mirrors including JobLeads, Jobrapido, BeBee and SmartJob. KitaLulus and Tech in Asia Jobs were checked for relevant current results but did not produce a verified survivor suitable for this batch.

Approximate candidate vacancies inspected: 82 posting candidates, including reposts and cross-portal variants before normalization.

## Phase 3 — second-pass quality audit

I reopened or rechecked the final source for every selected record and tried to disprove current availability by looking for a closed/expired label, a passed deadline, a dead application destination, or a stale duplicate. All 20 retained records showed a live application path or a current employer/listing page on the final pass. Four are aging enough to monitor immediately: Summarecon, Intelegensia, Koperasi Astra and Politeknik Astra. Summarecon has a visible 31 October deadline; the others should be rechecked at the moment of application.

Important audit removals:

- Cahaya Jakarta — Continuous Improvement Staff: Glints explicitly says the vacancy is closed, despite older JobStreet/search results.
- PT Patria Anugrah Sentosa — Business Process Staff: Glints explicitly says closed.
- Bank Permata — BAP Operations: Prosple says closed and the secondary deadline had passed; excluded.
- WINGS Trainee/Fast Track: university portal showed the August application window closed; aggregator hits were stale.
- PERURI internship: explicit closed state and June deadline; excluded.
- PT Fan Integrasi Technology — IT Business Analyst: Glints page says closed; excluded.
- Multipolar Technology — Business Analyst: one Dealls result looked active while a matching result said Closed; held out rather than guessing.
- Dipo Star Finance secondary mirror: the old Jobrapido URL returned 404 during the final check, so it was removed as a source; the current JobStreet search remains the primary source and the role is flagged to reconfirm before submission.

Existing PyoLoker data:

- HashMicro ERP System Analyst was excluded because a matching HashMicro ERP record already exists in the 27-job workspace.
- BCA Digital Business Analyst is an existing record whose current Dealls/LinkedIn source clearly says Closed. It was not changed.
- No production/owner records, applications, CV files or source statuses were mutated by this research pass.

## Limitations

Portal availability and employer listings can change after verification, especially for roles using aggregators or a generic search page. Some LinkedIn/JobStreet pages expose current status without exposing a stable employer requisition URL. Degree equivalence for Informatics against business/industrial-planning requirements remains a human decision. Salary is missing for most roles because the source did not publish it. No recruiter contact, form submission, auto-apply, scraping, or production mutation was performed.
